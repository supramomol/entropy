    Rules
    1. Every cell of the board starts with the same number of stones.
    2. One player names a number: 1, 2 or 3. It has to be a number of stones that can actually be moved.
    3. The other player moves exactly that many stones from one cell to a cell next to it (up, down, left or right). The cell receiving the stones must hold at least as many stones as the cell giving them.
    4. The player who just moved names the next number, and so on.
    5. Whoever empties a cell wins.
